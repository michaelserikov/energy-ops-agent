# Residual load
Residual load is load minus wind (onshore and offshore) minus solar generation, in MW. High residual load hours are served by dispatchable plants and imports, so prices follow it. In the stored data for January to March 2025 the day-ahead price rose by 3.1 EUR/MWh per GW of residual load (correlation 0.88); this is a descriptive slope, not a forecast rule.
