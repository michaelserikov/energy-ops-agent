# Dark doldrums (Dunkelflaute)
A dark doldrum is a period of low wind and low solar output, usually in winter, with high residual load and high prices. Example in the stored data: 12 December 2024 with mean price 395 EUR/MWh, maximum 936 EUR/MWh and wind output of 1.5 GW on average. The daily report flags such days because many hours are unusual against the previous 28 days.
